# Unit Economics: CredLens & Credex

CredLens operates as a "Zero-CAC" (Customer Acquisition Cost) engine for the Credex marketplace.

## 1. CredLens Operating Costs (The Lead Magnet)
Since the tool is a static frontend with a serverless backend:
- **Hosting:** $0 (Vercel Hobby/Pro tier).
- **Database:** $0 (MongoDB Atlas Free Tier).
- **AI Summary (Gemini):** ~$0.005 per audit (Input: ~1k tokens, Output: ~200 tokens).
- **Email (Resend):** $0 (First 3,000 emails/month free).
- **Estimated Cost per Lead:** <$0.01.

## 2. Credex Revenue Model (The Marketplace)
Credex acts as a wholesale aggregator for AI credits (OpenAI, Anthropic, AWS, etc.).

- **Wholesale Buy Rate:** Credex buys $1M+ in AI credits at a ~40% discount from vendors.
- **Retail Sell Rate:** Credex sells those credits to startups at a ~15% discount from public prices.
- **Gross Margin:** ~25% of every dollar spent through the platform.

### Example Scenario:
- **Startup Spend:** $2,000/mo on ChatGPT, Claude, and OpenAI API.
- **Public Price:** $2,000.
- **Credex Price (15% off):** $1,700.
- **Credex Wholesale Cost (40% off):** $1,200.
- **Monthly Profit per Startup:** $500.

## 3. LTV vs CAC
- **CAC:** Effectively $0 (driven by organic CredLens audits).
- **Payback Period:** Month 1.
- **LTV (12-month):** $6,000 profit per $2k/mo spending startup.

## 4. Scalability
As the number of CredLens audits increases, Credex gains more leverage to negotiate even higher bulk discounts (up to 50%) with vendors, further increasing margins while keeping prices low for the end user.
