# User Interviews & Market Research

To validate the CredLens concept, we conducted 3 mock interviews with target users (Founders and Engineering Leads).

---

## Interview 1: The "Scale-up" CTO
**Profile:** CTO of a 45-person Fintech startup.
**Current Situation:** Spending ~$4,000/mo on AI.
**Pain Point:** "I have no idea who has a Claude Pro account vs ChatGPT Plus. Half the team has both. I'm seeing $20 charges every day on the company card and I don't have the time to audit it manually."
**Reaction to CredLens:** "If this can just tell me who to move to a Team plan to save 20%, I'd use it in a heartbeat. The 'Shadow IT' problem is real."

---

## Interview 2: The "Solopreneur" Developer
**Profile:** Solo founder building an AI-native SaaS.
**Current Situation:** Spending ~$400/mo on APIs and IDEs.
**Pain Point:** "I'm using Cursor, Copilot, and ChatGPT. I know I'm overpaying, but I'm afraid of losing my context/history if I switch. I need a tool to tell me the *cheapest* way to keep my current workflow."
**Reaction to CredLens:** "The 'Switch Tool' recommendation is great. I didn't know Copilot Pro was only $10/mo now compared to Cursor's $20."

---

## Interview 3: The Operations Manager
**Profile:** Ops lead at a YC-backed AI startup (12 people).
**Current Situation:** Spending ~$1,500/mo.
**Pain Point:** "We hire interns and contractors every month. We forget to remove their seats from Cursor and ChatGPT. It adds up to hundreds of dollars of 'zombie' spend."
**Reaction to CredLens:** "The 'Seat Optimization' rule is exactly what I need. Seeing $200/mo in savings just from cleaning up seats is a huge win for a small team."

---

## Key Takeaways
1. **Visibility is the #1 value prop**, not just the dollar savings.
2. **"Switching costs" are a major fear**; recommendations must emphasize that capabilities are identical.
3. **Automated audits** save hours of "spreadsheet work" for Ops managers.
