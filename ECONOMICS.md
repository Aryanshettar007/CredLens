# Unit Economics: CredLens & Credex

CredLens is the zero-cost lead generator; Credex is the revenue engine. The goal is to acquire qualified, high-spend teams cheaply and convert them into credit buyers.

## Core Assumptions
- Average qualified lead spends $2,500/mo on AI tools and APIs.
- Credex sells credits at a 15% discount vs. retail and buys at a 40% discount.
- Gross margin on spend is ~25%.
- Average retained customer stays 12 months.

At $2,500/mo spend, gross margin is ~$625/mo. Twelve-month gross profit is ~$7,500 per customer.

## Funnel Math (Audit → Lead → Consult → Purchase)
Assume 1,000 audit completions in a month:
- 25% email capture rate = 250 leads
- 20% of leads are “high savings” and see the Credex consultation CTA = 50
- 30% consultation booking rate = 15 consults
- 35% close rate from consult to purchase = 5-6 customers

That is ~0.5% conversion from audit completion to purchase. With $7,500 12-month gross profit per customer, 1,000 audits produce ~$37,500 in 12-month gross profit.

## CAC by Channel (Time-Based, $0 Paid)
- Social + founder posting: 2 hours/week, 200 visitors/week. Effective CAC is labor only; at $50/hour, CAC is ~$0.50 per visitor and ~$2 per lead.
- Community posts + manual audits: 3 hours/week, 60 audits/week. At $50/hour, CAC is ~$2.50 per audit and ~$10 per lead.
- Product Hunt / Show HN launch: 10 hours prep, 1,500 visitors in launch week. CAC per visitor ~ $0.33, CAC per lead ~ $1.25.

Even with conservative labor pricing, CAC per lead stays under $10, which is dramatically below the ~$7,500 gross profit potential per customer.

## $1M ARR in 18 Months (Rough Math)
Target is $83,333/mo gross profit by month 18 (equivalent to $1M ARR in gross profit). Using the $625/mo gross profit per customer assumption:
- Required active customers = $83,333 / $625 ≈ 134 customers.

If 1,000 audits produce ~5-6 customers, you need ~25,000 audits over 18 months, or ~1,400 audits per month. That is achievable with consistent distribution, given the viral share loop and a weekly social cadence.

## Sensitivities
The model is most sensitive to two levers:
- **Lead capture rate:** every +5% lift adds ~1 extra customer per 1,000 audits.
- **Consult close rate:** improving from 35% to 50% increases customers by ~40% with the same traffic.
