# Landing Page Copy Variations

We will A/B test 3 different "Hooks" for the CredLens hero section to maximize audit completion rates.

---

## Variation A: The "Direct Savings" (Control)
**Focus:** Pure financial gain.
- **Headline:** Stop Overpaying for AI Tools.
- **Sub-headline:** Most startups waste 30% of their AI spend on unused seats and inefficient plans. Audit your stack in 60 seconds and find your hidden savings.
- **CTA:** Audit My Stack for Free →

---

## Variation B: The "Shadow IT" (Fear-Based)
**Focus:** Loss of control and hidden waste.
- **Headline:** Do You Know What Your Team is Spending on AI?
- **Sub-headline:** Shadow IT is costing you thousands. Every expensed ChatGPT account and forgotten Cursor seat adds up. Get a complete, tool-by-tool breakdown of your team's waste today.
- **CTA:** Start My Free Audit →

---

## Variation C: The "Smart CTO" (Value-Based)
**Focus:** Efficiency and optimization.
- **Headline:** The Smart Way to Build Your AI Stack.
- **Sub-headline:** Don't let your AI bill bloat your burn rate. CredLens uses advanced rules and AI to optimize your subscriptions so you can spend your budget on compute, not overhead.
- **CTA:** Get My Optimization Report →

---

## Testing Strategy
- **Traffic Split:** 33/33/33 via Vercel Edge Config.
- **Primary Metric:** Click-through rate (CTR) on the "Start Audit" button.
- **Secondary Metric:** Lead capture rate (Email entry) on the results page.
