# Success Metrics & KPIs

To measure the health and growth of CredLens as a lead generator for Credex, we track the following metrics.

## 1. Top of Funnel (Awareness)
- **Daily Unique Visitors (DUV):** Target 100+ unique hits per day post-launch.
- **Audit Start Rate:** % of visitors who click "Start Audit" (Target: >40%).

## 2. Engagement (Product Value)
- **Audit Completion Rate:** % of users who finish the form and land on the results page (Target: >85%).
- **Average Savings Found:** The $ amount of annual savings identified per user. A higher number increases the "Viral coefficient."

## 3. Conversion (Lead Gen)
- **Lead Capture Rate:** % of users who enter their email to unlock the detailed breakdown (Target: >25%).
- **Honeypot Bounce Rate:** % of submissions caught by our invisible spam field (Goal: <5%).

## 4. Monetization (Upsell to Credex)
- **Marketplace Click-through:** % of users who click the "Buy via Credex" CTA in their audit results (Target: 10%).
- **Onboarding Rate:** % of leads that successfully link their company card to the Credex wholesale marketplace.

## 5. Viral Growth
- **Share Rate:** % of users who click "Share Results" and generate a public `/share/[id]` URL.
- **K-Factor:** Number of new users generated by each existing user sharing their results. (Target: 0.1).
